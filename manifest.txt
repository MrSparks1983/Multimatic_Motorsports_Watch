NOTES=Persistent full-vehicle DBC profile release based on v1.8.43. The compact VEHDBC1 profile pushed by Motorsports Telemetry Manager is now saved to the watch's Preferences/NVS flash when VEH_DBC_COMMIT succeeds. The saved RPM, speed, gear, throttle, brake, steering, pressures, temperatures, lambda, battery, optional cooling channels and four-corner CAN TPMS mappings survive Windows disconnects, normal reboots and full power cycles. At boot the watch validates schema, stored payload size, mapping ranges and an FNV-1a checksum before activating the saved profile, then decodes raw MM-CAN-GW traffic locally with no Windows connection required. Profile updates are transactional: starting a replacement sync clears only the RAM staging profile; the previous flash profile remains intact until the new COMMIT is fully written. A failed or interrupted sync therefore cannot destroy the last known-good standalone profile. Corrupt or incompatible stored profiles are rejected and removed. v1.8.43 full VEHDBC1 mapping, Race Data CAN-driven refresh, compiled C1812 fallback, OTA, Crew, alerts and BLE recovery are retained.
