NOTES=Bluetooth runtime stability fix based directly on v1.8.29. Fixes the watch reboot that could occur when Bluetooth was turned OFF from the watch UI. The user-facing Bluetooth OFF path no longer calls BLEDevice::deinit(false), which was already known from the OTA handoff work to be unsafe on this ESP32-S3 runtime. Bluetooth OFF now performs a soft suspend: BF1 scanning stops, Settings discovery stops, companion advertising stops, and any active Watch Manager GATT link is gracefully disconnected while the BLE host/controller, services and characteristic objects remain allocated. Bluetooth ON resumes the existing stack, advertising and BF1 scanner without rebuilding them; a cold initialization path remains for boots where Bluetooth was disabled from startup. Advertising restart callbacks are explicitly blocked while soft-off is active. No GATT UUIDs, telemetry payloads, OTA protocol, units, alerts, power policy, diagnostics or UI layout are changed.
