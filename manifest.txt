NOTES=Bluetooth device-name discovery fix based directly on the confirmed-working v1.8.30 BLE soft-off baseline. Settings > Bluetooth discovery now temporarily uses an active BLE scan so ordinary peripherals that place their local name in the scan-response packet can be identified instead of appearing only as a MAC/generic device. A later packet containing a genuine local name upgrades the existing MAC entry regardless of RSSI, and the saved selected-target name is refreshed when the same MAC supplies a real name. After the Settings discovery pass completes, the scanner automatically restores the existing passive 160/80 BF1 scan configuration. The v1.8.30 soft-off stability behavior, companion GATT UUIDs, OTA paths, BF1 decoding, telemetry, UI, power behavior and protocols are otherwise unchanged.
